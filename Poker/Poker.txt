Functional Requirement:
-----------------------
- I'll implement a simple 5-card poker game: each player receives five cards, pays a fixed ante and can fold before the showdown.
- Players don't exchange cards, and there are no additional betting rounds. Those can be added once the core game works.
- Tied winners split the pot. Any leftover chip goes to the first tied winner in player order.
