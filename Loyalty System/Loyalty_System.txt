Functional Requirement:
-----------------------
- Points don't expire
- A customer's tier is updated automatically based on lifetime earned points.
- Every 100 points can be redeemed for a $1 discount.
- A customer cannot redeem more points than their current balance.
- In-memory implementation.

Core Entities:
--------------
- Customer
- LoyaltyAccount
- LoyaltyTier
- PointTransaction
- TransactionType
- LoyaltyProgram
- TierPolicy
- StandardTierPolicy
- InsufficientPointsException
